What happens after I mint?

Each NFT is a numbered on-chain room key. After minting, enter its number and sign a free access message. The room service checks current ownership before revealing its story. Room 000 is also available as a public sample.

How many can I mint?

1–100 per transaction. Repeat transactions while supply remains. Total supply: 5,555.

How does the free mint work?

2,222 free NFTs, one per wallet while the free supply lasts. There are also 3,333 paid NFTs at 0.00008 ETH each. Both mints open together. Additional mints from a wallet are paid. Network fees may apply.

Is minting live?

Connect your wallet to read the configured contract status. Mint requires a deployed contract and owner activation on Robinhood Chain mainnet. The website shows actual supply and exact quotes once configured. Private room access also requires the room server to be running.
